Snipkey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Summary

Snipkey does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal data. Your snippets stay in your browser.

What stays on your device

Your snippets and settings are saved with the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced or uploaded by Snipkey. Text you type is examined only inside the page to detect your triggers; it is never recorded or transmitted.

Payments and license keys

If you buy Snipkey Pro, payment is handled by our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy), which acts as the merchant of record and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. When you activate Pro, your license key is sent to the provider's license API to confirm it is valid, and is re-checked occasionally. No other data is sent.

Analytics

Snipkey contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code.

Contact

Questions: snipkey@gyomu-ai-sekkei.com
